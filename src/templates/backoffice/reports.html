How these are worked out

Quote-to-won rate
Quotations sent in the period that were accepted, divided by those that reached an outcome: accepted, declined, or expired unanswered. Quotations still open, and ones you withdrew, are left out so a busy week does not look like a bad one. "Of everything sent" is the stricter version.
Lead win rate
Leads received in the period now marked Won, out of those marked Won or Lost. Accepting a quotation marks its lead Won. Lost leads are deleted after the retention period, so very old periods look better than they were.
Time to close
Measured on quotations accepted in the period. "Lead to accepted" needs the quotation to be linked to a lead. "First payment to live" compares the first payment with the go-live date recorded on the hosted instance.
Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
The recurring price of every paying subscription expressed per month, as charged (VAT included). It is what is billed, not what has been received, and it leaves out the once-off first-payment extras. The excl. VAT figure appears once a VAT number is set.
Customer churn
Customers cancelled during a month, out of the customers who were paying at the start of that month. Revenue churn is the same measured in rand. The average is taken over months that started with at least one customer.
Received
Completed PayFast payments by the date they arrived, less refunds by the date they were processed.
Small numbers
With only a handful of leads or customers a single deal moves a percentage a long way. Read the counts beside each rate.
