What we collect and why

  • Enquiry form: your name, business name, email address, phone number, the size of your scheme, the plan you are interested in and any message you write. We use it only to answer your enquiry, arrange a demo and follow up on it.
  • Consent record: we record that you agreed, when, and which version of this notice applied.

Our staff and administrators

Everyone who signs in to our back-office to manage enquiries, quotations and customer accounts has a personal login. To keep your information secure and to show who did what, we record, for each of them:

  • their username, name and work e-mail address;
  • an audit entry every time they sign in, sign out or fail to sign in, and every time they change a record (for example sending a quotation or processing a refund), with the date and time and the IP address they were using;
  • for failed sign-ins, the username that was typed and the IP address, which is also used to block repeated guessing for a short time.
