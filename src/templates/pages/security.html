POPIA tools included in the Professional plan

  • Information Officer details and a privacy notice that shows them, with version and review dates.
  • Consent records for the privacy notice, parent or guardian consent for children, and marketing choices.
  • Data requests: access, correction, deletion and objection, with an answer-by date and identity check.
  • Retention clean-up that removes personal details once the retention period has passed.
  • Incident register to record security incidents and the decision on notifying the Information Regulator.
  • Policy-rule timers for cooling-off cancellations and claim decisions and representations.

Funera gives your team the tools to meet your obligations. It does not replace legal advice. Which rules apply to your business, and the exact periods to use, are for your compliance adviser to confirm.
